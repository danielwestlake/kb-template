# Overview

This knowledge base is a company-wide intelligence system, designed to turn the daily work of the business — proposals, meetings, support conversations, client feedback — into compounding, structured knowledge.

## Purpose

Most businesses accumulate knowledge in scattered places: email threads, Slack, shared drives, individual memories. This knowledge base changes that. Every source that comes in — a proposal, a meeting note, a support ticket — is integrated into a persistent, interlinked wiki that gets richer over time.

The four strategic focus areas:

1. **[[Client value]]** — What do clients need that they're not getting? Where are the expansion opportunities? What issues are building under the surface that a proactive intervention could address?

2. **[[Process improvement]]** — Where are the recurring failures? Which processes break down under load, cause client frustration, or eat team capacity? What are the highest-leverage fixes?

3. **[[Content marketing]]** — What genuine expertise and real outcomes can be turned into content that attracts new clients? What questions do prospects keep asking that deserve a public answer?

4. **[[Sales and growth]]** — What makes a proposal win? What are the patterns behind deals lost? How can the proposal process be faster, more consistent, and more persuasive?
